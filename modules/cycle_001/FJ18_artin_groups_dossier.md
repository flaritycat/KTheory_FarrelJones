## Definitions

### Definition. Artin group

Let \(\Gamma\) be a finite labelled simplicial graph with vertex set
\(V(\Gamma)\). The associated Artin group \(A_\Gamma\) has one
generator for each vertex. If two vertices \(s,t\) are joined by an
edge labelled \(m\), the defining relation identifies the alternating
word of length \(m\) beginning with \(s\) with the alternating word of
length \(m\) beginning with \(t\). Missing edges are treated as no
relation in the project-level notation.

This is a working definition for the dossier, following the conventions
used in the checked Artin-group sources.

### Definition. Even Artin group

An Artin group \(A_\Gamma\) is called even if all finite edge labels in
\(\Gamma\) are even.

### Definition. Right-angled Artin group

A right-angled Artin group is the Artin group associated to a simplicial
graph whose edge labels are \(2\). Equivalently, the relations are
commutation relations for adjacent vertices.

### Definition. Clique, standard parabolic subgroup, and FC-type

For \(T\subseteq V(\Gamma)\), \(A_T\) denotes the Artin subgroup
generated by \(T\), with full subgraph \(\Gamma_T\). A subset \(T\) is a
clique if any two vertices of \(T\) are joined by an edge. The
corresponding Coxeter group is denoted \(W_T\). An Artin group
\(A_\Gamma\) is of FC-type if \(A_T\) is of spherical type for every
clique \(T\) in \(\Gamma\).

### Source-level flag. FJCw

In Brueck--Kielak--Wu and Wu, `FJCw` means the K- and L-theoretic
Farrell--Jones conjecture with finite wreath products and coefficients
in additive categories.

Warning: this is stronger and more technical than the simplified
ring-coefficient K-theory statement from `FJ01`. The internal
additive-category foundations remain deferred to `FJ02`.

### Source-level flag. FICwF

In Roushon's notation, `FICwF` means the relevant isomorphism conjecture
with coefficients and finite wreath products. Roushon's checked theorem
is stated for K-, L-, and A-theories.

Warning: the present module only records the source-level theorem map.
It does not reconcile Roushon's `FICwF` notation with a canonical
project formulation.

## Main work

### Warning. No global Artin-group theorem is imported here

This module records several source-verified Artin-group subclasses. It
does not establish, import, or claim the Farrell--Jones conjecture for
all Artin groups.

Several sources raise reduction questions that would be useful for a
global Artin result, such as virtual poly-freeness or clique reductions.
Those questions are recorded here only as route information, not as
theorem inputs.

## Proposition / Theorem / Conjecture / Example

### Source-verified claim. First Artin-group Farrell--Jones subclass ledger

The Artin-group subclasses listed in the source-verified table above
are recorded as known Farrell--Jones cases only in the versions and
under the hypotheses stated in that table.

### Open problem. Global Artin-group status inside this project

The project has not established whether the currently checked sources
imply the Farrell--Jones conjecture for all Artin groups. The global
Artin-group case therefore remains unresolved inside this project.

## Proof or verification

This module is a literature-verification artifact, not an internal
proof.

Verification performed:

- Huang and Osajda's article page and theorem/corollary statements were
  checked for the FC-type Artin group and weak finite-type Garside
  group Helly result, and for the Farrell--Jones-with-finite-wreath-
  products corollary.
- Brueck--Kielak--Wu's arXiv source and bibliographic metadata were
  checked for Theorem A, Corollary B, and the right-angled Artin
  semidirect-product theorem.
- Wu's arXiv source and bibliographic metadata were checked for
  Theorems A, B, C, the definition of FC-type used there, and the
  FJCw convention.
- Roushon's article page, arXiv source, erratum, and corrigendum were
  checked for the listed Artin types, the `FICwF` convention, and the
  corrected \(\widetilde B_n\) proof route.
