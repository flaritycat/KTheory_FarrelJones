# Module FJ08. Whitehead group consequence

## Status

Completed, first pass

## Module type

Application / Theorem map / Literature verification

## Problem

Show how the K-theoretic Farrell--Jones conjecture implies vanishing of
the Whitehead group under suitable hypotheses, without erasing those
hypotheses.

The intended output is a conditional theorem map:

```text
torsion-free K-theoretic Farrell--Jones + regular coefficients
=> low-dimensional K-theory consequences
=> Whitehead cokernels vanish.
```

## Definitions

### Definition: regular ring

Following Lueck--Reich, a ring \(R\) is **regular** if it is Noetherian
and every \(R\)-module has a finite-dimensional projective resolution
(Lueck & Reich, 2004, Section 1.1).

Their examples include \(\mathbb Z\) and fields.

### Definition: the maps \(A_0\) and \(A_1\)

Let \(R\) be a ring and \(G\) a group. Let

\[
i\colon R\longrightarrow R[G]
\]

be the natural inclusion.

Lueck--Reich define

\[
A_0=K_0(i)\colon K_0(R)\longrightarrow K_0(R[G]).
\]

They also define a map

\[
\Phi\colon G_{\mathrm{ab}}\otimes_{\mathbb Z}K_0(R)\longrightarrow K_1(R[G])
\]

from the action of group elements on induced projective modules, and set

\[
A_1=\Phi\oplus K_1(i)\colon
G_{\mathrm{ab}}\otimes_{\mathbb Z}K_0(R)\oplus K_1(R)
\longrightarrow K_1(R[G]).
\]

Here \(G_{\mathrm{ab}}\) denotes the abelianization of \(G\).

### Definition: \(R\)-Whitehead groups

For \(R\) regular, Lueck--Reich define

\[
\mathrm{Wh}_1^R(G)=\operatorname{coker}(A_1)
\]

and

\[
\mathrm{Wh}_0^R(G)=\operatorname{coker}(A_0).
\]

For \(R=\mathbb Z\), the group \(\mathrm{Wh}_1^{\mathbb Z}(G)\)
coincides with the classical Whitehead group \(\mathrm{Wh}(G)\), described
as the quotient of \(K_1(\mathbb Z[G])\) by the subgroup generated by the
classes of the units \(\pm g\), \(g\in G\) (Lueck & Reich, 2004, Section
1.1).

Lueck--Reich also identify \(\mathrm{Wh}_0^{\mathbb Z}(G)\) with the
reduced group \(\widetilde K_0(\mathbb Z[G])\) in their notation.

## Main work

### Source-verified theorem map: torsion-free regular coefficients

Lueck--Reich formulate the low-dimensional consequence of the
K-theoretic Farrell--Jones conjecture as follows:

If \(G\) is torsion free and \(R\) is regular, then the conjectural
low-dimensional conclusion is

\[
K_n(R[G])=0\quad\text{for }n\leq -1,
\]

and the maps

\[
A_0\colon K_0(R)\to K_0(R[G])
\]

and

\[
A_1\colon G_{\mathrm{ab}}\otimes_{\mathbb Z}K_0(R)\oplus K_1(R)
\to K_1(R[G])
\]

are isomorphisms.

They state that the torsion-free all-dimensional assembly formulation
with regular coefficients implies this low-dimensional statement via the
Atiyah--Hirzebruch spectral sequence (Lueck & Reich, 2004, Conjecture
1.11 and the discussion following it).

Therefore, under these hypotheses and assuming the relevant K-theoretic
Farrell--Jones statement, the cokernels vanish:

\[
\mathrm{Wh}_0^R(G)=0
\]

and

\[
\mathrm{Wh}_1^R(G)=0.
\]

For \(R=\mathbb Z\), this gives

\[
\widetilde K_0(\mathbb Z[G])=0
\]

and

\[
\mathrm{Wh}(G)=0.
\]

### Relation to the project formulation

The project's simplified statement in `FJ01` uses the family
\(\mathcal{VCyc}\). In the torsion-free regular-ring setting,
Lueck--Reich record that the general Farrell--Jones formulation is
equivalent to the torsion-free version: for a torsion-free group \(G\),
the family of finite subgroups is the trivial family, and the virtually
cyclic family reduces to cyclic subgroups; regularity handles the
infinite cyclic comparison (Lueck & Reich, 2004, Proposition 2.10 and
Corollary 2.11).

This module therefore treats Whitehead-group vanishing as a consequence
of Farrell--Jones under the exact torsion-free and regularity assumptions
above.

### Warning: torsion-free is not cosmetic

Lueck--Reich explicitly warn that torsion-free hypotheses are necessary
for the low-dimensional integral-coefficient vanishing statement: finite
groups can have nonzero reduced \(K_0\) and nonzero Whitehead groups
(Lueck & Reich, 2004, Remark 1.4).

This module does not assert \(\mathrm{Wh}(G)=0\) for groups with torsion.

## Proposition / Theorem / Conjecture / Example

### Source-verified theorem map: Whitehead-group consequence

Let \(G\) be torsion free and let \(R\) be regular. Assume the
K-theoretic Farrell--Jones assembly statement in the torsion-free
regular-ring form used by Lueck--Reich. Then

\[
\mathrm{Wh}_0^R(G)=0
\qquad\text{and}\qquad
\mathrm{Wh}_1^R(G)=0.
\]

In particular, for \(R=\mathbb Z\),

\[
\widetilde K_0(\mathbb Z[G])=0
\qquad\text{and}\qquad
\mathrm{Wh}(G)=0.
\]

### Warning: conditional status

This is not a proof that every torsion-free group satisfies the
K-theoretic Farrell--Jones conjecture. It is a theorem map: once a
torsion-free group is known to satisfy the relevant Farrell--Jones
statement, the Whitehead-group conclusion follows under the stated
coefficient hypotheses.

## Proof or verification

The proof is formal after importing the source-verified statements.

By Lueck--Reich's low-dimensional formulation, the relevant
Farrell--Jones statement for torsion-free \(G\) and regular \(R\) implies
that \(A_0\) and \(A_1\) are isomorphisms. The groups
\(\mathrm{Wh}_0^R(G)\) and \(\mathrm{Wh}_1^R(G)\) are defined as their
cokernels. Hence both cokernels are zero.

For \(R=\mathbb Z\), Lueck--Reich identify
\(\mathrm{Wh}_1^{\mathbb Z}(G)\) with the classical Whitehead group
\(\mathrm{Wh}(G)\), and identify \(\mathrm{Wh}_0^{\mathbb Z}(G)\) with
\(\widetilde K_0(\mathbb Z[G])\). The integral-coefficient statement
follows.
