# Module FJ21. One-relator structure source selection

## Status

Completed

## Module type

Literature verification / Source selection

## Problem

Choose the canonical one-relator structure toolkit for `T-001`, the
torsion-free one-relator residual gap analysis selected in `FJ20`.

The project needs a source base before it can subtract known Farrell--Jones
routes from torsion-free one-relator groups. In particular, it needs a reliable
way to talk about Magnus subgroups, Magnus--Moldavanskii hierarchies, HNN
splittings, and later hyperbolic or free-by-cyclic routes.

This module uses:

- `FJ19`, the torsion-free one-relator groups dossier;

- source searches for one-relator hierarchy references.

## Output target

A ranked source toolkit for the next one-relator modules, with source-status
labels and exact cautions about proof-level use.

## Definitions

**Definition.** A one-relator structure source is a source used to organize
the group-theoretic structure of one-relator groups before applying
Farrell--Jones known-case or inheritance results.

**Definition.** A primary toolkit source is the source the project will follow
first for definitions, hypotheses, and theorem numbering in subsequent modules.

**Definition.** A bridge source is a source used to connect a modern paper,
survey, or theorem map to the project's current vocabulary without yet serving
as the proof source for every statement it mentions.

### Source-verified orientation facts

**Source-verified claim.** Linton's *Hyperbolic one-relator groups* records a
one-relator group in the form
\[
F(\Sigma)/\langle\langle w\rangle\rangle,
\]
where \(F(\Sigma)\) is the free group on \(\Sigma\). It defines a Magnus
subgroup as one generated by a subset of \(\Sigma\) omitting at least one
generator appearing in the cyclic reduction of \(w\).

**Source-verified claim.** The same source states the Freiheitssatz as:
Magnus subgroups of one-relator groups are free.

**Source-verified claim.** The same source describes the Magnus hierarchy as a
hierarchy in which a one-relator group splits as an HNN extension with a
one-relator vertex group of lower complexity and edge groups that are Magnus
subgroups.

**Source-verified claim.** Linton's *One-relator hierarchies* develops a
refinement of the classical Magnus--Moldavanskii hierarchy and introduces
\(\mathbb Z\)-stable HNN extensions and \(\mathbb Z\)-stable hierarchies.

**Source-verified claim.** Linton and Nyberg-Brodda's survey is a 2025 arXiv
survey of one-relator group theory divided into historical development and
recent progress. For this project, it is useful as a map of the literature, not
as a proof source for a theorem unless a later module checks the relevant
section in detail.

### Selection

**Selection.** The project will use Linton's *One-relator hierarchies* as the
primary modern hierarchy toolkit for `T-001`.

**Selection.** The project will use Linton's *Hyperbolic one-relator groups* as
the bridge source for `FJ21` and `FJ22`, because it gives a compact entry point
to Magnus subgroups, the Freiheitssatz, one-relator towers, primitive extension
groups, and the modern hierarchy vocabulary.

**Selection.** The project will use Linton and Nyberg-Brodda's survey as an
orientation source for deciding which classical and modern papers to verify
next.

**Warning.** Lyndon--Schupp and Magnus--Karrass--Solitar are not yet active
proof sources inside this project. They are recorded as classical sources to
verify before the project uses classical theorem numbering proof-sensitively.

**Warning.** Brown's BNS-invariant paper and Louder--Wilton's negative
immersion paper are not yet active inputs for a Farrell--Jones reduction. They
are downstream sources for later modules, especially free-by-cyclic and
hyperbolicity-route subtraction.

## Proposition / Theorem / Conjecture / Example

**Selection.** The canonical first-pass one-relator structure toolkit for
`T-001` is:

1. Linton, *One-relator hierarchies*, as primary modern hierarchy source;
2. Linton, *Hyperbolic one-relator groups*, as the immediate bridge source;
3. Linton--Nyberg-Brodda, *The theory of one-relator groups: history and recent
   progress*, as orientation survey;
4. Lyndon--Schupp and Magnus--Karrass--Solitar as classical sources to verify
   before proof-sensitive use.

**Warning.** This is a source-selection result, not a mathematical theorem and
not an established result number.

## Proof or verification

The selection is justified by the following checks.

1. Linton's *Hyperbolic one-relator groups* was checked through the Cambridge
   article page. The page gives the published bibliographic data and records
   the relevant orientation statements on Magnus subgroups, Freiheitssatz, and
   the HNN-splitting hierarchy picture.
2. Linton's *One-relator hierarchies* was checked through arXiv. The abstract
   identifies the source as a refinement of the classical
   Magnus--Moldavanskii hierarchy and introduces the \(\mathbb Z\)-stable HNN
   and hierarchy vocabulary. Later modules must check the exact theorem
   statements before using them as proof inputs.
3. Linton--Nyberg-Brodda's survey was checked through arXiv. The abstract
   identifies it as a survey with a historical chapter and a recent-progress
   chapter. It is appropriate as a source map, not as a replacement for primary
   theorem checks.
4. Lyndon--Schupp, Magnus--Karrass--Solitar, Brown, and Louder--Wilton were
   bibliographically located or source-located for later verification.
