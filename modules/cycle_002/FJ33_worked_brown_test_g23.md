## Definitions

**Definition.** Let
\[
G_{2,3}=\langle x,y\mid x^2y^{-3}\rangle .
\]
Let \(\chi\colon G_{2,3}\to\mathbb Z\) be defined by
\[
\chi(x)=3,\qquad \chi(y)=2.
\]

**Definition.** In the semidirect-product form below, let \(F(p,q)\) denote
the free group on two generators \(p,q\).

**Warning.** This module proves a concrete route bridge for \(G_{2,3}\). It
does not generalize the bridge to all two-generator one-relator groups, all
torus-knot-style presentations, or all of `RB-004`.

## Main work

### Brown test

Use the cyclically reduced relator
\[
r=xxy^{-1}y^{-1}y^{-1}.
\]

The proper initial segments and \(\chi\)-values are:

| \(i\) | \(s_i\) | \(\chi(s_i)\) |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | \(1\) | 0 |
| 2 | \(x\) | 3 |
| 3 | \(x^2\) | 6 |
| 4 | \(x^2y^{-1}\) | 4 |
| 5 | \(x^2y^{-2}\) | 2 |

The maximum is \(6\), and it occurs exactly once.

For \(-\chi\), the values are:

| \(i\) | \(s_i\) | \(-\chi(s_i)\) |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | \(1\) | 0 |
| 2 | \(x\) | -3 |
| 3 | \(x^2\) | -6 |
| 4 | \(x^2y^{-1}\) | -4 |
| 5 | \(x^2y^{-2}\) | -2 |

The maximum is \(0\), and it occurs exactly once.

Since \(\chi(x)\) and \(\chi(y)\) are both nonzero, Brown's Theorem 4.3 uses
the condition that the maximum occur exactly once. Thus both \([\chi]\) and
\([-\chi]\) pass Brown's test. Brown's Corollary 3.2 therefore gives that
\(\ker(\chi)\) is finitely generated.

### Change to braid presentation

Define
\[
a=y^{-1}x,\qquad b=x^{-1}y^2.
\]
Then
\[
ab=y,\qquad aba=x.
\]
Also,
\[
bab=x^{-1}y^2y^{-1}xx^{-1}y^2=x^{-1}y^3=x.
\]
Therefore \(aba=bab\), and the presentation is equivalent to
\[
G_{2,3}\cong \langle a,b\mid aba=bab\rangle.
\]
Under \(\chi\), one has
\[
\chi(a)=1,\qquad \chi(b)=1.
\]

Conversely, from \(\langle a,b\mid aba=bab\rangle\), set
\[
x=aba,\qquad y=ab.
\]
Since \(aba=bab\),
\[
x^2=(aba)(bab)=(ab)^3=y^3.
\]
Thus the change of presentation is reversible.

### Semidirect-product bridge

Now work with
\[
G_{2,3}\cong \langle a,b\mid aba=bab\rangle .
\]

Set
\[
t=a,\qquad p=ba^{-1}.
\]
Then \(b=pt\), and the braid relation becomes
\[
tpt^2=pt^2pt.
\]

Add
\[
q=tpt^{-1}.
\]
Then \(t^2pt^{-2}=tqt^{-1}\), and the braid relation is equivalent to
\[
q=p(tqt^{-1}),
\]
or
\[
tqt^{-1}=p^{-1}q.
\]

Hence
\[
G_{2,3}\cong
\langle p,q,t\mid tpt^{-1}=q,\ tqt^{-1}=p^{-1}q\rangle.
\]

Let \(\varphi\colon F(p,q)\to F(p,q)\) be defined by
\[
\varphi(p)=q,\qquad \varphi(q)=p^{-1}q.
\]
This is an automorphism. An inverse is
\[
\psi(p)=pq^{-1},\qquad \psi(q)=p.
\]
Therefore
\[
G_{2,3}\cong F(p,q)\rtimes_\varphi\mathbb Z,
\]
where the \(\mathbb Z\)-factor is generated by \(t\).

Since
\[
\chi(t)=1,\qquad \chi(p)=\chi(q)=0,
\]
the kernel of \(\chi\) is exactly \(F(p,q)\cong F_2\).

### Target-status bridge

The original presentation is a one-relator presentation. The semidirect form
\[
F_2\rtimes_\varphi\mathbb Z
\]
also proves torsion-freeness internally: if \((u,n)^m=(1,0)\), then
\(mn=0\), so \(n=0\), and then \(u^m=1\) in the free group \(F_2\), forcing
\(u=1\).

Thus \(G_{2,3}\) is a torsion-free one-relator group for the purposes of
the project target `T-001`.

### FJ26 route decision

The module has now recorded:

1. Brown-positive checks for \([\chi]\) and \([-\chi]\);
2. an exact sequence
   \[
   1\to F_2\to G_{2,3}\to\mathbb Z\to 1;
   \]
3. a splitting \(G_{2,3}\cong F_2\rtimes_\varphi\mathbb Z\).

Therefore \(G_{2,3}\) satisfies the finite-rank free-by-cyclic bridge
required by `FJ26`. The `FJ26` route may be invoked for this concrete
example.

## Proposition / Theorem / Conjecture / Example

**Proposition.** For
\[
G_{2,3}=\langle x,y\mid x^2y^{-3}\rangle
\]
and \(\chi(x)=3,\chi(y)=2\), one has
\[
\ker(\chi)\cong F_2
\]
and
\[
G_{2,3}\cong F_2\rtimes_\varphi\mathbb Z.
\]

**Proof.** The Brown maximum-count computation above verifies both
\([\chi]\) and \([-\chi]\), so Brown's Corollary 3.2 gives finite generation
of \(\ker(\chi)\).

The change of variables
\[
a=y^{-1}x,\qquad b=x^{-1}y^2
\]
identifies \(G_{2,3}\) with \(\langle a,b\mid aba=bab\rangle\), with
\(\chi(a)=\chi(b)=1\). Setting \(t=a\), \(p=ba^{-1}\), and
\(q=tpt^{-1}\) gives
\[
G_{2,3}\cong
\langle p,q,t\mid tpt^{-1}=q,\ tqt^{-1}=p^{-1}q\rangle.
\]
The map \(p\mapsto q\), \(q\mapsto p^{-1}q\) is an automorphism of
\(F(p,q)\), with inverse \(p\mapsto pq^{-1}\), \(q\mapsto p\). Hence the
presentation is \(F(p,q)\rtimes\mathbb Z\), and the kernel of \(\chi\) is
\(F(p,q)\cong F_2\).

**Example.** The group \(G_{2,3}\) is removed from the active `T-001`
residual by the finite-rank free-by-cyclic route of `FJ26`.

**Proof.** The proposition gives
\[
G_{2,3}\cong F_2\rtimes_\varphi\mathbb Z.
\]
This is exactly the finite-rank free-by-cyclic bridge recorded in `FJ26`.
The known-case input is the hyperbolic-by-cyclic/free-by-cyclic route
recorded there from Bestvina, Fujiwara, and Wigglesworth (2023).
