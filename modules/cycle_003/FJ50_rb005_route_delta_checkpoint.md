## Definitions

**Definition.** In this module, `RB-005 local bridge exhaustion` means that
the currently named finite-index bridges have been checked far enough to
decide that none presently gives a `T-001` residual subtraction.

**Definition.** A `bounded pivot` is a move from one residual bucket to
another with a named next module and a stop condition, not a general search
for interesting sources.

**Warning.** Pausing `RB-005` is not a theorem that no finite-index route
exists. It is a project-state decision: the currently recorded inputs do not
justify another adjacent finite-index source check.
